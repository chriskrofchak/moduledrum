Moduledrum is installed

To make sound, Moduledrum needs Elektron_SPS1-1UW_OS1.63.syx, the Machinedrum OS 1.63 from Elektron's website (Machinedrum support / downloads; unzip the download). Open Moduledrum in your host, click Select OS File... and choose it. You only do this once; every Moduledrum instance shares the setting, and the file stays where you put it.

If your host does not list the plugin, rescan its plugins. Logic Pro scans Audio Units at launch.
